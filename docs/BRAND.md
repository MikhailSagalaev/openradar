# openradar: name and voice

## Name

The project is always written **openradar**, in lowercase: in the interface, the docs, the
reports, the extension, posts and quotes. Not "OpenRadar" and not "Open Radar".

- Citing the research: «по данным openradar», «индекс openradar».
- At the start of a sentence the name stays lowercase: «openradar собирает вакансии…».
- The domain is openradar.fun; the repository is `openradar`.

Code identifiers, environment variables (`RADAR_*`) and package names keep their own rules.

## Voice

Plain and exact. Figures come with their sample size and period; a claim the data cannot
support is not made. The research describes what employers and clients offer in published
posts, not what people earn.
